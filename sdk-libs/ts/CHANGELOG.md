# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha — unreleased

The custom ring surface of `@heliuslabs/zolana` moves into
`@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings`, built on `@heliuslabs/zolana` as a pinned
peer. `CustomRingClient` adds the ring proofs and the ring indexer reads to a
`ZolanaClient`. Custom rings keep members, lists, escrowed keys and their
policy in a member registry with a record queue, prove transfers on a
confidential, an anonymous and a delegate rail, and let an auditor open every
slot under the layout the ring program verified.

Breaking

- Mint thresholds, outflow limits and withdrawal co-sign thresholds move from eight-entry arrays into authenticated asset records → write `MintPolicy` through `AuthorityListWrite.withMintPolicy(memberOfAsset(mint), policy)`. `RuleTable` carries rules only and `setRingCoSignerInstruction` takes signer and scope only. Upgrade the program, SDK, indexer and keys together for fresh rings; existing rings need an explicit state migration.
- `RingMode` names the confidential modes `"confidential"` and `"confidentialEscrow"` in place of `"off"` and `"registry"`, matching Rust `RingMode::Confidential` and `RingMode::ConfidentialEscrow`. The config byte is unchanged → compare `config.mode` with the new names.
- Transfers use instruction tags 43 and 44 and policy statement version 2. Public withdrawals now follow output-owner and asset list rules, including gross amount guards; direct deposits and same-owner merges keep their existing policy behavior. SPL owner snapshots are checked at execution and exposed as `screenedOwner` in historical audit results. Legacy transfer frames remain readable, but cannot execute on the upgraded program.
- Anonymous transfer, entry and merge proofs bind the actual fee payer, configured co-signer and public withdrawal owner. Proof requests and proving-key pins change; deploy the program, prover and SDKs together after publishing the pinned key artifacts.
- Policy keys specialize by registry sources and withdrawal capacity. Deposits use capacities 1, 2, 4 and 8; registry updates use 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16. Clients select the smallest matching key.
- `@heliuslabs/zolana/ring` and the custom ring, auditor and key registry
  exports of the `@heliuslabs/zolana` entry points move here → import them from
  `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings` or its `client` and `keypair` entry points.

Fixed

- An anonymous transfer read the first contact's path before approval and from an indexer behind the slot it was staged at, now it reads the path only once approved, an indexer at or past that slot that withholds a pinned leaf or answers malformed fails with `RING_ANCHOR_INVALID`, and one that stays behind fails with `CLIENT_INDEXER_NOT_CAUGHT_UP`.
- An anonymous transfer took an address slot, a note of another owner than the sender, or a default-ring note carrying data into proof assembly, now it refuses the address slot with `RING_ANONYMOUS_SLOT_INVALID` and the foreign note with `RING_MIXED_OWNERS` before any proof, and an entry funds from default-ring notes without data, a zero data hash counting as none.
- `auditRing` opened a note twice when the sender's notes named it twice under one prefix, now each note opens once.
- `RingKeyError.rustVariant` named Rust variants that do not exist for the stream and disclosure codes, now it names each with its enum, as in `StreamError::IndexOutOfRange`.
- A hand-built `RuleTable` skipped the checks `decodeRuleTable` runs, now policy writes, `policyTableProofInputs` and its hash rerun `buildRuleTable` and refuse such a table with `RING_RULE_TABLE_INVALID`.
- `CustomRingProver.proveRing` and `ringProofRequestBody` threw a raw `TypeError` or `InterfaceError` on a request of an unknown kind or with a slot list that is not an array, now they fail with `CLIENT_INVALID_PROOF_INPUTS` before any request is sent.
- `RingRpc` accepted an incomplete, repeated or oversized signed audit page and `auditRing` misjudged where a scan ends, now such a page fails, a last page naming neither a next cursor nor a scan frontier fails with `RING_SCAN_FRONTIER_MISSING`, a terminal page at the page limit completes the scan, an invalid page limit fails, and each `DecryptedRingTransaction` names its `eventIndex`.
- `RpcTransactionOrigin` refused a confirmed transaction that loads an address lookup table, which stopped `auditRing` and ring recovery, now `confirmedInstructionGroups` reads its loaded accounts after the static keys and still refuses loaded accounts that differ from the lookups its message names.
