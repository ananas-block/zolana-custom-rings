# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha — unreleased

The custom ring surface of `@heliuslabs/zolana` 0.4.0-alpha moves into `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings`, which takes `@heliuslabs/zolana` as a pinned peer, and `CustomRingClient` adds the ring proofs and ring indexer reads to a `ZolanaClient`. A ring holds its rules and role keys in one `RingPolicy` account, its lists and mint settings in Storage Program records and each owner's nullifier key in a tracking registry, and it runs in the `confidential` or the `anonymous` mode. Every ring operation sends as one transaction whose auditor stream opens under its transaction viewing key.

Breaking

- `@heliuslabs/zolana/ring` and the ring, auditor and key registry exports of the `@heliuslabs/zolana` entry points move here → import them from `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings` or its `client`, `keypair` and `advanced` entry points.
- Ring instructions, accounts, proofs and frames change format, `RingProgramError` drops `staleKeyRegistryRoot` and `RingErrorCode` drops the codes of the removed key registry, list entry, spend record and velocity paths → deploy this release's ring program, Storage program, prover, keys and Ring RPC together and re-register each ring under its newly derived auditor key.
- `ZolanaClient`'s `proveCustomRing*`, `proveIndexedRingPolicy`, `proveIndexedRingDeposit`, `getRingSpendRecord`, `getRingKeyRegistryEntry` and `getRingKeyRegistryRegisterProof`, `proveCustomRingTransfer`, `proveCustomRingDelegateTransfer`, `provePolicyAnswers`, `ringDelegateTransactInstruction` and `CompressedProof.toCustomRingProof()` are gone → create the client with `createCustomRingClient(config)`, implement `proveRing` in a custom `RingProver`, and convert a prover proof with `customRingProof(proof)`.
- `initializeRingInstruction` replaces `initializeRingConfigInstructions`, `createRingConfigInstruction`, `initSppRingConfigInstruction`, `createRingPolicyInstruction` and `buildRingCreatePolicyTransaction`, `updateRingPolicyInstruction` replaces `setRingPolicyRulesInstruction`, `setRingPolicySourceInstruction` and their transaction builders, `updateRingAuthorityInstruction` replaces `setRingAuthorityInstruction`, `fetchRingCoSignerRecord` replaces `fetchRingCoSigner`, and `fetchRingPolicy` replaces `fetchRingProgramConfig`, `fetchRingPolicyConfig`, `fetchRingConfigs` and `fetchRingDelegate` → create the records registry first, pass four distinct nonzero `roles` and the rule table to `initializeRingInstruction`, appoint each co-signer with `setRingCoSignerInstruction`, and pass the account `fetchRingPolicy` returns as `current` to `updateRingPolicyInstruction`.
- `buildRingListWriteTransaction`, `grantReadAccessInstruction`, `revokeReadAccessInstruction`, `fetchReaderGrant`, the list entry tree with `createRingEntryInstruction`, `updateRingEntryInstruction` and `readRingEntries`, and spend registration with its spend records and spend windows are gone, and `buildRuleTable` and `decodeRuleTable` refuse a mixed polarity row, a row over several absences, an inverted polarity and a rule over the `reader` list with `RING_RULE_TABLE_INVALID` → write lists, reader grants and each mint's caps and windows in its `MintPolicy` record with a signed `WalletRecordUpdate` or `AuthorityRecordUpdate` sent through `RingRpc.submitRecordUpdate`, and rewrite each refused rule.
- `registerRingKeyInstruction`, `buildRingKeyRegistrationTransaction`, `prepareRingKeyRegistration` and the other key registry exports are gone and `ListId` drops `recovery` and `escrow` for `privateBlock` and `privateAllow` → enroll each owner's nullifier key with `enrollInstruction` and `proveEnrollment`, pay an enrolled owner at its `RingAddress`, and pass `listSalts` naming every enrolled owner a salted list reads, since a spend without one raises `RING_LIST_SALT_MISSING`.
- The ring spend builders take `cosigners` in place of `cosigner` and no `computeUnitLimit`, the `RING_*_COMPUTE_UNIT_LIMIT` constants are gone, and a spend over more than two input trees raises `RING_TOO_MANY_INPUT_TREES` in place of `RING_MULTIPLE_INPUT_TREES` → pass a co-signer for every class the ring demands and read an instruction's limit with `ringComputeUnitLimit(tag)`.
- `RingDepositClient` needs `getStorageRecordPool` and `getTrackingRecordPool`, every deposit seals its note to the ring's auditor, and `setRingDepositAuditInstruction` and `fetchRingDepositAudit` are gone → give the deposit client both reads and drop the deposit audit setup.
- `auditRingTransaction` takes the `layout` of a `ConfirmedRingTransaction`, and `AuditorEncryption`, `encryptTransactionViewingSecret`, `decryptTransactionViewingSecret`, `openAuditOutputDisclosure` and `openRingEscrowedKeys` are gone → implement `confirmedTransaction` in a custom origin and open an auditor stream with `openRingDisclosure`.
- `syncWallet` no longer opens ring deposits and `recoverRingMemberNotes` returns a `RingRecoveryStep` → sync ring wallets with `syncRingWallet`, drop `depositPayloadDecoder` from the `syncWallet` config, and pass the `progress` of a `paused` step back until a step is `complete`.
- `RingRpc` posts each JSON-RPC method beneath its base URL, takes `RingRpcOptions.apiKey` or the URL's `api-key` and leaves the key out of `RingRpc.url`, and every signed read takes a `RingReadTarget` → forward method paths unchanged, supply the key in one place, and pin the Ring RPC service key in each `RingReadTarget`.

Added

- The `anonymous` ring mode hides each spend's owner, `AnonymousSync` and `AnonymousWallet` scan and hold a wallet's ring notes under a `ViewingHistory`, a `RingAddress` or `RingPaymentRequest` names a recipient, and the `anonymous` rail of the spend builders proves the hidden owner.
- `prepareRingTransferRelay`, `prepareRingExitRelay`, `prepareRingEntryRelay`, `prepareRingWithdrawalRelay`, `prepareRingMergeRelay` and `prepareEnrollRelay` send an operation through the Ring RPC relay, the `create*Submission` builders prove and send one with no relay, and `TransactionFit` and `decideAddressCeiling` refuse a transaction past 4,096 bytes or 64 accounts before any proof.
- `CoSignerApproval`, `OwnerApproval` and `FunderApproval` let a co-signer, a public owner and a public funder check their own part of a relayed transaction, and `ProvenRingTransfer.chargedOutflows` lists each owner's charged outflow per asset before signing.
- `buildRingDelegateRecoveredTransaction` moves notes of several members on the delegate rail from openings Ring RPC serves only to the ring's delegate, and `PreparedAuthorityTransfer` signs a relayed move only when it spends notes `AllowedNullifiers` admits for the requested sources.
- `RingRpc.getWalletStatus`, `getWalletActivity` and `getSpendCoverage` read a wallet's notes, its history and a ring's unresolved spends, `ringFullScan` reads a whole ring with no tag read, and `RingRpc.setMergeSettings` with `RingMergeSettingsRequest` sets the note count Ring RPC merges a mint toward.
- `AnonymousWallet.exposedNotes` holds zero-key notes apart from the balance, no automatic selection spends one, a shortfall they could cover raises `RING_UNFUNDED_PAST_EXPOSURE`, and `CustomRingTransfer.spendPublic(wallet)` spends the ones the caller names.
- `rotateViewingKey` moves a wallet's `LocalRingKeys` and `ViewingHistory` to a new viewing key, `updateViewingKey` builds the proofless `Enroll` that writes it into the owner's tracking authorization, and `confirmViewingKey` raises `RING_VIEWING_KEY_NOT_PUBLISHED` until the authorization of an applied enrollment holds it.

Fixed

- A ring entry from a default note larger than its amount asked approval for the amount alone while the change also entered the ring, now the `ringEntry` intent and its summary name every unit entering the ring.
- `recoverRingMemberNotes` failed on one output whose mint no registry names, now that commitment stays in `unopened` and recovery goes on.
